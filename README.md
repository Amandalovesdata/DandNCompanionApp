# Dough & Nourish Companion

[Download the Android app](https://github.com/Amandalovesdata/DandNCompanionApp/releases/latest/download/Dough-and-Nourish-Companion.apk)

1. Open the download link on your Android phone. When the download finishes, tap the APK and choose **Install**. If Android asks for permission to install from your browser, follow the prompt to allow this installation.
2. Open the app and sign in with your Dough & Nourish course account.
3. Allow notifications when prompted so you can hear your course timers.

This download is for phones with a 64-bit ARM processor. For future versions, use the same download link and choose **Update** when Android asks.
